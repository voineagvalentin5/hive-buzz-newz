France: school blockades shrink but violence at high schools hardens the government's line

Education Minister Edouard Geffray said 24 high schools (0.5%) were blockaded on Thursday morning, down from 49 on Tuesday, while condemning what he called unbridled violence. The prime minister's office ordered systematic prosecution of offences in and around schools, Le Figaro reports mortar fire and fires at some schools, and new protests are called for Saturday Nov 14 and a public-sector and CGT strike on Tuesday Nov 17.

Sources: Ouest-France live · Le Figaro · La Provence

Confirmed

Spain: unions and employers give wildly different verdicts on the general strike

CCOO put turnout at up to 84% in Catalonia and around 70% in several regions, while employer group CEOE said average turnout was below 5% and called the strike a failure. The government delegation counted 120,000 marchers in Madrid (the tenants' union said 400,000), and Storm Charles forced marches in several Andalusian cities to be called off.

Sources: EFE · Euronews (Spanish) · El Independiente · elEconomista
