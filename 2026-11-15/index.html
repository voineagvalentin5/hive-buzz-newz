France & Europe

Confirmed

France: fifth Saturday of protests draws fewer people, with clashes on the fringes

The Interior Ministry counted 106,000 marchers at 137 events on Nov 14, including 24,000 in Paris, down from 146,000 a week earlier; youth unions put the total at 200,000. The ministry reported 219 people detained and 10 police officers lightly injured, and local media reported police using tear gas in cities including Marseille, Lyon, Nantes and Bordeaux (Al Jazeera). Unions have called a new strike day for Tuesday, Nov 17, after rejecting a 0.6% minimum wage rise as 'crumbs'.

Sources: Al Jazeera · French Interior Ministry · La Provence · France 24

Developing

Belgium: De Wever's last-chance budget meeting before he reports to the King

Prime Minister Bart De Wever called his deputy prime ministers to a final core-cabinet meeting at 2 pm Brussels time (8 am ET) on Sunday, Nov 15, ahead of his report to King Philippe on Monday. Le Soir reports that four of the five coalition parties appeared willing to negotiate on his revised plan, with Vooruit more reserved, and a full deal by Monday looks unlikely.

Sources: BelgiumNow · Le Soir

Confirmed

Albania: student-led protest in Tirana ends after five hours with fires and damage

On day 168 of the protests, students and citizens rallied outside the Education Ministry on Nov 14, blocked traffic and marched to the prime minister's office, demanding Edi Rama's resignation. Rubbish bins and advertising signs were set on fire, railings were damaged and some protesters tried to break the door of the parliament speaker's office. Organisers plan national protests every Wednesday and Saturday at 2 pm.

Sources: Albanian Telegraphic Agency · Albanian Telegraphic Agency (earlier report) · Vox News Albania

Confirmed

Romania: more than 10,000 rally in Bucharest for detained Calin Georgescu

The far-right AUR party held an unauthorised protest near the Palace of Parliament on Nov 14 in support of former presidential candidate Calin Georgescu, who is being held at Bucharest's central detention facility. More than 2,000 people then gathered outside the central detention facility, and the protest ended without major incidents.

Sources: G4Media · Mediafax

Confirmed

Germany: 'Gen Z regelt' youth march in Berlin ends with arrests

Police said about 1,200 people joined Berlin's youth protest on Nov 14, against 100 registered. Police said 20 people were detained (an earlier figure was 22) and 28 criminal complaints were filed.

Sources: rbb24 · Tagesspiegel (dpa) · tagesschau

Confirmed

Georgia: 48th 'Saturday March' in Tbilisi, bigger rally called for Nov 28

Thousands marched in Tbilisi on Nov 14 under the slogan 'Georgia against Ivanishvili', demanding a return to EU integration, the release of political prisoners and early elections. Organisers announced a large march for Nov 28.

Sources: JAMnews · InterPressNews

Developing

Serbia: no deal on opposition posts before the new parliament sits on Nov 17

Consultations on Nov 13 ended without agreement on whether the opposition gets any deputy speaker posts. The Student List, which holds 140 of 250 seats, says the speaker and deputies will be chosen by majority vote, while Vucic's SNS says opposition rights are being cut.

Sources: The Serbian Review · B92

Confirmed

Spain: first campaign weekend turns into a war of words

Pedro Sanchez campaigned in Palma on Nov 14, appealing to pro-European voters and urging Alberto Nunez Feijoo to accept the result of the Nov 29 election. Feijoo, campaigning in Santiago de Compostela, summed up the government as 'corruption, machismo, incompetence and arrogance' and promised a million homes.

Sources: RTVE · RTVE campaign diary · laSexta

Confirmed

Ukraine: Russian drone attack hits Odesa region's power grid

Russia launched 132 drones overnight into Nov 15, of which Ukraine says it stopped 106. A strike on a DTEK energy site in Odesa Oblast cut power to about 41,000 families, six people were injured in the region and buildings were damaged in Chornomorsk; Ukrainian officials say Russian attacks killed four people and injured 69 across the country over the past day.

Sources: Kyiv Independent · LB.ua

The government and the main trucking unions agreed to cut diesel by 100 pesos a litre and reverse this week's 42-peso rise, about 142 pesos in total, and to freeze diesel prices until March 31, 2027, ending the blockade by those unions, while some other groups said they would keep mobilising.

US & Canada

Confirmed

US: copper-wire upgrade blamed for the nationwide flight stop

Transport Secretary Sean Duffy said Friday's four-hour ground stop began after a router being worked on during a copper-to-fibre upgrade by contractor L3Harris flooded other air traffic systems. Flight trackers counted more than 5,900 cancellations and 7,000 delays on Saturday, and the FAA expects schedules back to normal by the end of Sunday. Officials say there is no sign of a cyber-attack and are still investigating whether the wire work or the router was at fault.

Sources: BBC · Aviation Week

Confirmed

Canada: Montreal police use tear gas on student cost-of-living protest

A student march over education, food and housing costs turned violent in downtown Montreal on Nov 13. Police said projectiles were thrown and made three arrests, and student associations will vote later this month on whether to call an unlimited general strike.

Sources: CBC News · CityNews Montreal · Le Devoir

COP31 ends its first week with many issues pushed to ministers
