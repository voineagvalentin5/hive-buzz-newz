France

Developing

France: unions, students and pensioners strike and march nationwide

Five unions joined the student-led cost-of-living movement for a national day of strikes on Thursday, with intelligence services expecting 500,000 to 700,000 marchers outside Paris. About 70,000 police and gendarmes are deployed, schools and regional trains are disrupted, and Paris marchers were sent on a République-to-Nation route after a court upheld a ban on the unions' preferred route.

Sources: Reuters · RFI · BBC

Developing

France: high school set on fire near Toulouse as blockades continue

France Télévisions reported that masked people set fire to a high school in Tournefeuille near Toulouse and students were evacuated. The education ministry counted 81 high schools blockaded by mid-morning, while the main student union claimed about 1,000 were shut.

Sources: franceinfo

Confirmed

France: lawmakers unanimously reject the 2027 budget

The National Assembly voted down the revenue half of the 2027 budget 458 to 0 on Wednesday night, which sinks the whole bill at first reading. The government's original text now goes to the Senate, which starts work on it on November 20.

Sources: Assemblée nationale · franceinfo · Euronews
