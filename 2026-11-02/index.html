France: school blockades restart, but on a small scale

As classes resumed after the autumn break, 41 high schools (about 1% nationwide) were blockaded on Monday morning, in Paris, Marseille, Rennes, Toulouse and the north. The government calls blocking schools illegal, and student unions have called more blockades for Tuesday and Thursday.

Sources: France 24 · Al Jazeera · franceinfo

Confirmed

France: Macron's office says it's time to go back to class

The Élysée said on Sunday that the time had come to return to class. The government has already offered to replace the Parcoursup admissions system by 2028, cancel planned education job cuts, set a 28-pupil class cap and spend €4 billion on school repairs. PM Lecornu meets student groups again next week, but unions still want €10 billion and Parcoursup scrapped.

Sources: TF1 Info · franceinfo

France: final Oct 31 tally is 779 arrests

The Interior Ministry counted 779 arrests, 159 injured security officers and 515 fires from the Halloween protests. Turnout of about 320,000 was down 22% on the week before.

Sources: DW · AP

Germany: Gen Z protest fallout after Frankfurt looting

Around 100,000 to 120,000 young people marched in more than 30 cities on Oct 31 over living costs and their future. Frankfurt saw looting, fires and firebombs, and police used water cannon in Cologne and Hamburg.

Sources: NBC News · bne IntelliNews

Switzerland: Halloween riots in Zurich and Basel

Rioters set bins, e-bikes and a car alight around Zurich's Langstrasse, and police answered with water cannon, tear gas and rubber bullets. Two men were arrested, Basel also saw clashes, and Zurich politicians condemned the violence.

Sources: swissinfo · SRF
