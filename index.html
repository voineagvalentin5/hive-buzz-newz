France & Europe

Developing

France: students, university staff and police unions mobilise on Nov 26

An inter-union group of student and higher-education unions called a national day of strikes and marches on Nov 26, saying the government's answers remain insufficient; the Paris march was set to leave Bastille at 14:00 local time for the Panthéon. Four police unions called on officers to gather outside police stations at midday to press for a bigger rise in their risk allowance, which Alliance wants at 35%; BFM TV reports slower border checks were expected at Orly airport. AFP, via France 24, says the youth movement has spread out since its October peak, with 20% to 25% of higher-education sites disrupted according to the ministry, while Unef's leader says protests will resume in January if there are no new government announcements before Christmas.

Sources: franceinfo (French) · BFM TV (French) · France 24 / AFP (French)

Confirmed

France: about 90 rallies mark the day against violence against women

Rallies were held in around 90 places across France on Nov 25, the International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women, including a NousToutes event at Place de la Bastille in Paris, with protesters calling for the Senate to pass the comprehensive law against sexist and sexual violence that the National Assembly adopted on Oct 13. More than 1,000 people marched in Tours, according to La Nouvelle République. Further marches are planned in Lille, Lyon and Paris on Saturday, Nov 28.

Sources: Le Progrès / AFP (French) · La Nouvelle République (French)

Confirmed

Surprise Eurostar strike cancels Paris-London trains

An unannounced strike by Eurostar staff on Nov 25 led to cancellations and delays between Paris and London; Eurostar said it was told of the action overnight. Euronews describes the strikers as Paris-based staff, including drivers and managers, and counted at least six cancelled trains and five delayed, while The Connexion reports staff in Paris and Brussels walked out. A union source linked the strike to pay demands during annual negotiations, according to The Connexion; passengers with cancelled trains were offered free rebooking or a full refund.

Sources: Euronews · The Connexion

Developing

EU summit opens with leaders far apart on the 2028-2034 budget; Macron defends the farm envelope

EU leaders began an extraordinary summit in Brussels on Nov 26 on the bloc's next long-term budget, close to €1.6tn for 2028-2034, and a €45bn loan to Ukraine; Italy's Giorgia Meloni said they were "light-years away" from a solution, and Council president António Costa said he hoped for a compromise by the end of the year. On the eve of the summit, Emmanuel Macron told French farm unions that France wants its full €66.25bn of farm-policy funding, and an adviser said continuing the talks beyond December did not appear possible if they failed then. Spain's Pedro Sánchez is skipping the summit ahead of Sunday's election and has delegated his vote to Macron, POLITICO reports.

Sources: The Guardian (live) · France 24 / AFP (French) · POLITICO Europe

Developing

Belgium: Vooruit sets two conditions as coalition widens the budget target to about €13bn

After De Wever met the five coalition party leaders on Nov 24, CD&V's Sammy Mahdi said the savings-and-revenue target was now around €13bn instead of €10bn, and the leaders plan to meet again this weekend; De Wever told parliament on Nov 25 the goal was "a few billion" more but would not confirm the figure. At a press conference on Nov 25, Vooruit leader Conner Rousseau kept his party in government but demanded that wage and benefit indexation be protected and that millionaires make an extra contribution; VRT reports that if the other four parties refuse, Vooruit says they will have to look for another majority.

Sources: VRT NWS · VRT NWS · Belga News Agency

Developing

Spain: polls close the gap before Sunday's election as the publication ban starts

Spain votes on Nov 29, and Spanish law bars publishing polls in the final five days. El País's polling average put the PP at 31.5%, the PSOE at 29% and Vox at about 18%, with the PSOE narrowing the gap during the campaign, while the state-run CIS survey is an outlier giving the PSOE 34.4% to the PP's 24.7%, according to Las Provincias. Pedro Sánchez faces Alberto Núñez Feijóo, Santiago Abascal and Ada Colau in the campaign's final debate on Thursday evening, POLITICO reports.

Sources: El País (English) · Las Provincias (Spanish) · POLITICO Europe

Confirmed

Serbia sets Dec 27 presidential election to choose Vučić's successor

Newly elected parliament speaker Aleksandar Čupić signed the decision on Nov 26 setting Serbia's presidential election for Dec 27. Aleksandar Vučić resigned as president in September to run for prime minister in the Oct 25 parliamentary vote, which candidates nominated by the student-led protest movement won in a landslide. The presidency is mostly ceremonial, though the president proposes the prime minister-designate.

Sources: Reuters, on The Straits Times · The Guardian (live)

Developing

Ukraine: ballistic missiles hit a Kyiv shopping centre; 34 countries and the EU tell Russia to stop using teargas

Russian ballistic missiles struck a shopping centre, an industrial building and a non-residential building in Kyiv's Desnianskyi district late on Nov 25; the Kyiv Independent, citing the city military administration, says seven people were hurt, while the Guardian's earlier briefing cited Mayor Klitschko as saying three were wounded. Ukraine's general staff said a strike on Savasleyka airbase put three MiG-31s and two Su-34s out of action "for some time", a claim Russia did not immediately address. Officials from 34 countries and the EU urged Russia to stop using riot-control agents after the OPCW said approved labs confirmed CS in samples from a trench Kyiv said was hit by a Russian grenade; Russia denies using illegal munitions.

Sources: The Guardian · Kyiv Independent

Developing

Poland plans a no-fly zone along its eastern border

Deputy Defence Minister Cezary Tomczyk said on Nov 25 that his ministry will ask the government to set up a no-fly zone stretching from a dozen to a few dozen kilometres from the eastern border, to make it easier to identify and shoot down Russian missiles and drones; he hopes it can be in place by the end of December or early January. Tomczyk said NATO opposes shooting down missiles over Ukraine, an idea Foreign Minister Radosław Sikorski again raised, and opposition PiS figure Mariusz Błaszczak called the plan cosmetic.

Sources: Notes from Poland · The Guardian

Middle East

Developing

Hormuz: Iran claims more tankers and an LNG carrier hit mines; claims not independently verified

In a statement carried by Iranian state media on Nov 24-25, Iran's Revolutionary Guards navy said two oil tankers using an "unauthorized route" hit naval mines and caught fire; later the Iranian army, via Fars, said another tanker hit a mine in a "restricted zone", and the Guards said that evening that an LNG carrier had hit a mine and was burning. Reuters, via Arab News, said it could not independently verify the LNG claim; Iran International reports the Joint Maritime Information Center called earlier tanker-mine claims unconfirmed, while UKMTO said three ships were hit by unidentified projectiles on Nov 23 and kept sailing. An Iranian army spokesman said surveillance of the strait had been upgraded, and Tehran says normal shipping will not resume until its demands, including lifting the US blockade, are met.

Sources: Reuters, on Arab News · Iran International · The Hill

Developing

Houthis claim strikes on Saudi Arabia's Ras Tanura refinery and Riyadh; Saudi-led coalition reports interceptions

Houthi spokesman Yahya Saree said on Nov 25 that attacks on Ras Tanura, Saudi Arabia's largest refinery, caused fires; the Saudi-led coalition said it downed four drones there and did not say the refinery was hit, though The National says footage appeared to show smoke at the site. The coalition also reported minor damage at Jizan port, a drone downed over Jeddah port and ballistic missiles intercepted on the way to Riyadh, after the Houthis threatened Saudi ports over strikes on Yemen's Hodeidah port. Saudi civil defence said 11 people were hurt on Nov 24 when a tower in Riyadh's financial district was hit.

Sources: Al Jazeera · DW · The National

Confirmed

Gaza: Israeli strike kills a senior Hamas security official and his family

An Israeli strike on an apartment in Gaza City's Shati refugee camp on Nov 25 killed Mohammed Abu al-Ouf, 47, head of Hamas's internal security service in northern Gaza, along with his wife and their daughters aged 15 and 13, according to Gaza's Hamas-run interior ministry; Israel's military called him a significant figure and said it followed the killing of his commander, Naeem Abu Nahel, a day earlier. Al Jazeera reports at least seven Palestinians were killed in Israeli attacks that day. Gaza's Hamas-run health ministry says at least 1,679 people have been killed since the October 2025 ceasefire, the BBC reports.

Sources: BBC · Al Jazeera

Africa

Developing

Sudan: RSF drone strikes kill at least 14 to 19 in Blue Nile state

Drone strikes on Damazin, capital of Blue Nile state, and nearby Roseires late on Nov 24 killed at least 14 people, according to a hospital source quoted by AFP, while the rights group Emergency Lawyers puts the toll at 19, including six women, with 20 wounded. Emergency Lawyers says power facilities and the Roseires dam were also targeted. AFP says the RSF has advanced toward Damazin, and the UN says more than 100,000 people from surrounding villages have fled into the city.

Sources: Al Jazeera · AFP, on Arab News

Confirmed

South Africa: court sets aside the Phala Phala report behind Ramaphosa's impeachment process

The Western Cape High Court on Nov 25 declared unlawful and set aside the 2022 independent panel report that found President Cyril Ramaphosa may have a case to answer over the theft of cash from his Phala Phala farm, and also set aside the steps parliament took under it. The court said the panel went beyond its mandate and made errors of law; the BBC reports opposition parties have indicated they intend to challenge the ruling. Ramaphosa has denied wrongdoing.

Sources: BBC · eNCA

Asia-Pacific

Developing

Indonesia: 13 dead and 13 missing after an illegal gold mine floods

Heavy rain flooded an unauthorised gold mine in the Pongkor area of Bogor, West Java, on Nov 23, trapping 26 workers about 100 metres underground; rescuers have recovered 13 bodies and 13 people remain missing, officials said on Nov 26, according to Al Jazeera; Reuters, via India Today, still described the 13 as trapped. About 325 rescue personnel are working in narrow, dark and slippery shafts. State miner Antam, which operates in the area, says the site was not part of its operations.

Sources: Al Jazeera · India Today (Reuters)

Latin America

Confirmed

Colombia: police storm a Cartagena supermarket and free all hostages after a 17-hour siege

Colombian security forces stormed an Éxito supermarket in Cartagena early on Nov 25, ending a siege that began when armed men fled there after a failed attempt to rob an armoured cash vehicle; all hostages were freed, four suspects were arrested and one man police described as a hostage-taker was killed. President Abelardo de la Espriella first spoke of 30 hostages, but police later put the total at about 70, the Guardian reports. Local media identified the dead man as a 51-year-old whose wife had been searching for him as a hostage and who reportedly had no criminal record; the mayor said he resisted and attacked an officer.

Sources: The Guardian · Euronews

US & Canada

Confirmed

Nor'easter postpones Macy's 100th Thanksgiving parade to Saturday

Macy's moved its 100th Thanksgiving Day Parade in New York from Thursday to Saturday, Nov 28, because of a powerful coastal storm; it is the first time the parade has been postponed for weather. New York Governor Kathy Hochul declared a state of emergency for New York City, Long Island and Westchester, and New Jersey Governor Mikie Sherrill declared one statewide. A high wind warning is in effect for the city from 10 a.m. Thursday, with gusts that could top 60 mph, plus heavy rain and coastal flooding.

Sources: BBC · weather.com

Markets & Business

Developing

US proposes new tariffs on 14 economies over 'excess capacity'; Asia pushes back

The US Trade Representative found on Nov 25 that 14 economies, including China, the EU, Japan, India and Vietnam, have practices that contribute to structural manufacturing overcapacity, and proposed tariffs such as 20% for China and 15% for the EU, with written comments due Jan 5 and hearings from Jan 25, 2027. USTR Jamieson Greer told CNBC the rates mostly work as a top-up and would not take effect for a couple of months. On Nov 26 China said it reserved the right to take "necessary measures", Japan called the plan regrettable and Singapore said it was very disappointed, while Vietnam, Malaysia and Thailand sought better terms, CNA reports.

Sources: USTR · CNBC · CNA

Confirmed

Oil jumps after the Houthi refinery claim; US stocks mixed before Thanksgiving

Brent crude rose 3.5% to settle at $94.61 a barrel on Nov 25 and US crude rose 2% to $83.68 after the Houthis said they had attacked Ras Tanura, CNBC reports. Yahoo Finance reports the Dow ended up about 0.3% while the S&P 500 fell about 0.2% and the Nasdaq about 0.6%, as chip stocks slid and core PCE inflation came in at 3.3%; the BBC said all three main indexes closed lower. US stock markets are closed for Thanksgiving on Nov 26 and reopen for a half-day on Friday.

Sources: CNBC · Yahoo Finance · BBC

Brazil: storms kill six in Paraná as a cyclone threatens more

Heavy rain since Nov 24 has killed six people in Paraná and forced more than 2,600 people from their homes, with damage in at least 50 towns, according to G1 citing civil defence. DW Brasil reports at least eight deaths across Paraná and São Paulo states, citing local media counts, and says the weather service warned that an extratropical cyclone could bring new storms to Paraná with winds of up to 100 km/h.

Sources: G1 (Portuguese) · DW Brasil (Portuguese)
