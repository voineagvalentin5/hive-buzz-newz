France & Europe

Developing

Albania: Independence Day protest in Tirana turns violent outside ministries

Thousands of people, including students and members of the diaspora, filled Skanderbeg Square in Tirana on Nov 28, Albania's 114th Independence Day, demanding Prime Minister Edi Rama's removal, a caretaker government and free elections; Portal Analitika reported more than 2,500 police on duty. The state news agency ATA says the square was calm for about three hours, but once the crowd marched toward the prime minister's office, masked protesters threw Molotov cocktails at the Ministry of Infrastructure and Energy and stones at the Interior Ministry, and police responded with water cannon and tear gas. ATA says the opposition Democratic Party backed the protest without its party symbols. The situation was still unfolding at the time of this check.

Sources: ATA (Albanian) · Sot News · Portal Analitika (Montenegrin)

Developing

France: seventh Saturday of marches, with Paris police expecting about 40,000

Feminist groups, unions, young people and gilets jaunes have called marches across France on Nov 28, especially against violence against women, France 24 reports, in what it calls the seventh Saturday in a row of protests since the movement began. In Paris two separate marches start at 2 pm local (8 am ET), and the police prefecture expects about 40,000 people, more than last Saturday. Youth organisations had also called marches for Saturday after the Nov 26 student protests. This check found no new statement by Emmanuel Macron on the protests.

Sources: France 24 (French) · AFP, on Midi Libre (French)

Confirmed

France slips into technical recession after GDP is revised down

France's statistics office INSEE said on Nov 27 that the economy shrank 0.2% in the third quarter, worse than its first estimate of 0.1%, Reuters reports. INSEE also revised the second quarter slightly lower, to a 0.02% fall that still rounds to zero, which Reuters says technically puts France in recession. Reuters adds that this makes the government's 0.5% growth target for 2026 all but out of reach as it struggles to pass its 2027 budget.

Sources: INSEE (French) · Reuters, on Investing.com

Developing

Spain: campaign closes before Sunday's snap election

Spain's party leaders held their final rallies on Nov 27 before the Nov 29 general election. The BBC reports Pedro Sánchez said "I cannot imagine us losing" while Alberto Núñez Feijóo's PP is narrowly ahead in the polls and promised a new political era.

Sources: BBC · RTVE (Spanish)

Confirmed

Spain: 14 migrants found dead on a boat off El Hierro

At least 14 people died on a boat carrying more than 100 migrants that was found on Nov 27 about 72 km south of El Hierro in Spain's Canary Islands, Spanish officials said. Six people were airlifted to hospital in serious condition and 84 were brought ashore at La Restinga. RTÉ notes arrivals to the Canaries hit a record 2,100 people in a single day on Nov 25.

Sources: Al Jazeera · RTÉ

Developing

Ukraine: Nizhyn strike toll rises to seven as Russia and Ukraine trade mass drone attacks

Ukraine's State Emergency Service said on Nov 28 that seven people were confirmed killed in a Russian strike on an industrial site in Nizhyn, Chernihiv region, on Nov 27, up from three, RFE/RL reports, and Zaporizhzhia's governor said one person was killed and five injured over the past day. Ukraine's air force said Russia launched 162 attack drones overnight into Nov 28 and 141 were shot down or jammed, and five people, including a child, were injured in Dnipropetrovsk region. Russia's defence ministry said it downed 1,246 Ukrainian drones overnight, and Moscow mayor Sergei Sobyanin said more than 400 were launched toward the capital, RTÉ reports; the Kyiv Independent reports a fire near the Novoshakhtinsk oil refinery in Rostov region, which it could not immediately verify, and Bryansk's governor said a drone hit a bus, wounding nine.

Sources: RFE/RL · RTÉ · Kyiv Independent · NV · Ukrainska Pravda

Developing

Ukraine offers a Black Sea ceasefire to protect grain exports

Foreign Minister Andrii Sybiha said on Nov 27 that Ukraine has sent Russia a proposal for a ceasefire in the Black Sea to restore free navigation and keep farm exports moving, and is waiting for a reply. Al Jazeera reports Moscow wants oil shipments included, which Kyiv says belongs in a wider energy ceasefire.

Sources: Al Jazeera · RFE/RL

Developing

UK: US forces repelled about six drones at RAF Lakenheath on Nov 13, FT reports

US forces used counter-drone systems to repel about half a dozen drones that swarmed RAF Lakenheath in Suffolk on Nov 13, the Financial Times reported on Nov 28, citing people familiar with the matter who said Iran was suspected; the drones were not recovered. Britain's Ministry of Defence confirmed reports of unauthorised drone activity that day and said the situation was quickly resolved.

Sources: CNBC / Reuters · GB News

Confirmed

Italy passes school law banning face coverings and capping foreign pupils

Italy's Chamber of Deputies gave final approval to the school decree by 142 votes to 112 with four abstentions, Euronews reports. It bans face coverings such as burqas and niqabs in school activities, with fines of €200 to €1,000, and caps pupils of foreign origin at 30% of first-year classes, except those who already speak Italian well; opposition MPs called it discriminatory.

Sources: Euronews · ANSA (Italian)

Developing

Georgia: marches tonight mark two years of protests

Protesters plan to gather at 7 pm local (10 am ET) on Nov 28 at Tbilisi State University and at Avlabari metro, then march to Freedom Square, IPN reports. The rally marks two years since Prime Minister Irakli Kobakhidze suspended EU accession talks, which set off daily protests demanding new elections and the release of detainees.

Sources: IPN · IPN

Developing

Belgium: Vooruit still threatens to skip Monday's budget meeting

Vooruit leader Conner Rousseau still makes his party's attendance at Monday's coalition budget meeting conditional on guarantees over wage indexation and a millionaires' tax, 21News reports, and De Morgen says Prime Minister Bart De Wever is trying to win Vooruit over during the weekend. 21News says De Wever has set Dec 31 as the deadline for a budget deal.

Sources: 21News (French) · De Morgen (Dutch)

Middle East

Confirmed

US disables another ship leaving Iran as Tehran vows to keep Hormuz shut

US Central Command said aircraft fired precision munitions into the stern of the Palau-flagged Pacific Archer in the Gulf of Oman after it left Bandar Abbas, disabling its propulsion without harming the crew; DW says the blockade has now disabled six ships and redirected 158. Mohammad Mokhber, an adviser to Iran's supreme leader, said on Nov 27 that the Strait of Hormuz would not be reopened and Iran would not let the blockade stay in place, Reuters reports via Al-Monitor.

Sources: DW · Reuters, on Al-Monitor

Developing

Kuwait summons Iran's envoy; Rome opens murder probe into Italians' deaths

Kuwait summoned Iran's ambassador over the Nov 26 drone strike that killed two Italian technicians at a Kuwaiti military site, DW reports, adding that a Kuwaiti and a Pakistani were also injured. Rome prosecutors opened an investigation, including for murder, into the deaths of Antonio Saccoccio and Emanuele Lo Russo, LaPresse reports. Iran's foreign minister Abbas Araghchi called the accusations baseless and offered a joint investigation with Kuwait and Italy.

Sources: DW · LaPresse · Euronews

Confirmed

Gaza: Israeli strike on an MSF clinic kills two staff

Médecins Sans Frontières said an Israeli strike on Nov 27 was a direct hit on one of its clearly marked clinics in Gaza City, killing two watchmen and bringing its staff killed in Gaza to 17. The Israeli military said the two men were Hamas operatives acting as security guards, and WHO chief Tedros called the attack "outrageous and unacceptable". Al Jazeera reports Israeli attacks elsewhere in Gaza killed four Palestinians on Friday, and a son of Hamas leader Khalil al-Hayya survived a strike on his family.

Sources: BBC · Al Jazeera · Al Jazeera

Developing

Yemen: coalition strikes hit at least six governorates; deaths reported in Taiz and Sanaa

Al Jazeera's correspondent in Sanaa said the Saudi-led coalition hit more than six governorates overnight into Nov 28, with 13 people injured when a gas station in Sanaa's Tahrir district was hit, and the Houthis said another strike killed a man and his wife. Houthi-affiliated media said a strike on Nov 27 killed 11 civilians, mostly children, in al-Turbah in Taiz province, with eight bodies recovered; Al Jazeera could not verify this and the coalition did not immediately comment. The coalition said it destroyed a Houthi drone heading for the Riyadh region.

Sources: Al Jazeera · Al Jazeera

Africa

Developing

Sudan: rights group says RSF attacks killed at least 14 in East Darfur and West Kordofan

The Sudan Human Rights Observatory said on Nov 27 that Rapid Support Forces attacks killed 12 people in East Darfur, where villages in Yassin locality were attacked for three days, and two in West Kordofan, Anadolu and Vatican News report. This toll comes from a single rights group and has not been independently confirmed.

Sources: Anadolu · Vatican News

Confirmed

DR Congo's cobalt export halt starts Monday

Congo's strategic minerals regulator ARECOMS will suspend all cobalt exports from Nov 30 until further notice to tackle oversupply and weak prices, Reuters reported. It is the second halt since April 2025, and CRP Africa says the country mined about three-quarters of the world's cobalt.

Sources: Reuters, on CNBC Africa · CRP Africa

Latin America

Developing

Venezuela to repeal law barring opposition figures from office

National Assembly chief Jorge Rodríguez said on Nov 26 that Venezuela will repeal a 2024 law that bars people who backed sanctions from holding office, Al Jazeera and the BBC report. It comes a month after opposition leader María Corina Machado returned to the country.

Sources: Al Jazeera · BBC

Confirmed

Mexico files a complaint with the US over a DEA tip line

President Claudia Sheinbaum said on Nov 27 that Mexico lodged a formal complaint with the US ambassador over a Drug Enforcement Administration tip line seeking information on suspected traffickers and alleged corruption among Mexican officials, Al Jazeera and Mexico News Daily report.

Sources: AP and Reuters, on Al Jazeera · Mexico News Daily

US & Canada

Confirmed

Canada's Supreme Court largely upholds Quebec's religious-symbols law

In a 6-1 ruling on Nov 27, Canada's Supreme Court largely upheld Quebec's Bill 21, which bars some public-sector workers from wearing religious symbols at work, finding Quebec could shield it from most charter challenges. Chief Justice Richard Wagner wrote that the law is rooted in Quebec's "legitimate and historical commitment" to state secularism.

Sources: CBC · The Guardian

Developing

US: hundreds of thousands still without power after the Thanksgiving storm

Anadolu, citing PowerOutage.us, said more than 400,000 US customers were still without power, down from 656,377, with Pennsylvania worst hit, and that Virginia's governor declared a state of emergency after New Jersey and New York. Al Jazeera, also citing PowerOutage.us, said outages peaked above 900,000 on Thursday and had fallen to 264,000 by Friday evening, with Pennsylvania still the worst-hit, followed by Missouri. Anadolu cited FlightAware figures of at least 2,489 delays and 144 cancellations; Al Jazeera cited about 1,600 delays and more than 500 cancellations.

Sources: Anadolu · AFP and AP, on Al Jazeera

Markets & Business

Confirmed

Wall Street posts its best week since April 2025; Brent ends the week 3% higher

US stocks rose in Friday's shortened session, with the S&P 500 up 1.6% to 7,297.49, the Nasdaq up 2.31% and the Dow up 1.18%, led by chipmakers; the S&P 500 gained 5.6% for the week, Reuters reports. Brent fell 1.2% on Friday to $95.44 a barrel but rose 3% for the week, its first weekly gain in November, on worries over Gulf supplies, CNBC reports.

Sources: Reuters, on Investing.com · CNBC

Vietnam: nearly 1,700 homes flooded as heavy rain hits the centre

Vietnam's civil defence committee ordered emergency measures and evacuation plans in six central provinces and cities from Hue to Khanh Hoa, Tuoi Tre reports.

Sources: VnExpress · Tuoi Tre
