France & Europe

Confirmed

France: Act VI turnout falls again; clashes in several cities and 144 arrests

The Interior Ministry counted 82,500 marchers across France on Nov 21, including 21,500 in Paris, down from 106,000 a week earlier; organisers said 150,000 nationally and 45,000 in Paris. The Paris march from République to Nation was calm apart from some tension at the end, while the ministry reported damage, bin fires and attempted looting by mobile far-left groups in cities including Marseille, Lyon, Nantes and Bordeaux, and 144 arrests by 6 pm local time. Reuters described the day as giving the government some respite after weeks of protests.

Sources: French Interior Ministry (French) · RFI (French) · Reuters, on Internazionale

Confirmed

Portugal: CGTP march fills central Lisbon despite heavy rain

The CGTP union federation marched from Saldanha to Restauradores on Nov 21, a day after a public-sector strike, demanding higher wages and pensions and controls on the prices of essentials. The CGTP put turnout at tens of thousands on its own site and 'many thousands' to Reuters; police gave no estimate. The union called a rally at parliament on Nov 24, the day of the final budget vote.

Sources: Reuters, on The Straits Times · CGTP-IN (Portuguese) · RTP (Portuguese)

Confirmed

Ireland: thousands march in Dublin to keep the 'Triple Lock' on troop deployments

Thousands marched from the Garden of Remembrance to Leinster House on Nov 21 against government plans to drop the UN Security Council mandate from the rules for sending 12 or more troops abroad. Opposition parties including Sinn Féin and Labour took part; the government says neutrality is not affected, and the bill goes to a parliamentary committee next week.

Sources: RTÉ · The Irish Times

Developing

Albania: another Saturday of student protests in Tirana, with bins set alight

Students and citizens gathered outside the Education Ministry on Nov 21 and marched toward the prime minister's office, repeating that Edi Rama's resignation is non-negotiable. Police with water cannon stopped them blocking Durrës Street, and some protesters set bins on fire and clashed with a police cordon. A nationwide protest is called for Nov 28.

Sources: Albanian Telegraphic Agency · CNA (Albania)

Developing

Belgium: De Wever and Vooruit's leader hold three-hour talks as the budget stalemate drags on

PM Bart De Wever met Vooruit president Conner Rousseau for three hours on Friday evening, Nov 20; the meeting only became known on Saturday and what was said is not known. Vooruit wants cuts to social security and health care matched by taxes on the highest earners. A political scientist told RTL a way out is still possible but a return to elections is a real risk.

Sources: RTL Info (French)

Developing

Spain: polls a week before the Nov 29 election still favour Feijóo

A SocioMétrica poll for El Español gives the PP 31.8% and 132 seats against 28.5% and 113 for Sánchez's PSOE, with PP and Vox together on 199 of 350 seats. A Sigma Dos poll for El Mundo finds 58.5% expect Feijóo to be the next prime minister versus 26.1% for Sánchez, though the two are close on who people would like to win.

Sources: El Español (Spanish) · El Mundo (Spanish)

Confirmed

NATO's Rutte vows to defend 'every inch' of Poland; allies meet on Nov 23

In Warsaw on Nov 21, Mark Rutte called the Nov 20 entry of two Russian cruise missiles and a drone into Polish airspace a 'dangerous incident' and said NATO's top commander is checking for gaps in its defences. The North Atlantic Council meets in Brussels on Monday, Nov 23, on the incident, and Poland says it expects strong support for protecting its eastern border.

Sources: Kyiv Post · Vatican News

Developing

Ukraine: 14 missing after a Russian drone destroys part of an apartment block near Kyiv

A drone strike on Nov 22 brought down part of a five-storey building in Dymer, north of Kyiv; Zelensky said 14 people were unaccounted for, and regional officials said one body had been recovered and at least three people hurt. In Kryvyi Rih the toll from the previous night's attack rose to two dead and 73 injured. Zelensky said Russia fired about 2,700 drones, 1,460 aerial bombs and 165 missiles this week and urged faster US approval of Patriot interceptors.

Sources: Euronews · La Repubblica (Italian)

Middle East

Developing

Hormuz: more tankers hit as Bahrain says Iran struck an industrial site

UKMTO said two tankers were hit in the Strait of Hormuz, one by an 'unknown projectile', with no casualties reported, and Iran's navy said a tanker caught fire after hitting a mine in a restricted zone; the US military says it has cleared mines from the main routes. Bahrain said an Iranian attack set a civilian industrial facility on fire, while the IRGC said it targeted US positions in Bahrain. Foreign Minister Araghchi said talk of reopening the strait is pointless until Iran's demands are met.

Sources: RTÉ (AFP) · Gulf Today · CNBC

Confirmed

US denies Iran's claim that a missile damaged an F-16

Iran's army said on Nov 21 that an air-defence missile exploded near a US F-16 the evening before and forced an emergency landing. US Central Command called the claim false, saying Iran did not attack an F-16 at all and all US fighter jets in the region are safe and accounted for.

Sources: Anadolu Agency · The Jerusalem Post

Confirmed

Gaza: drone strike on a food-aid van kills three as displaced residents protest demolitions

At least four Palestinians were killed on Nov 22, three of them when a drone hit a van delivering food in Gaza City, Al Jazeera reports. Hundreds of displaced people protested near the 'Yellow Line' against the destruction of homes; Gaza's Health Ministry puts deaths since the October 2025 ceasefire at 1,661.

Sources: Al Jazeera · Anadolu Agency

Premium Times reports that Kambari militias then attacked Fulani residents in nearby villages, with witnesses counting about 20 bodies in Wawa alone.

Sources: Premium Times · Punch

Masked gunmen stormed a yard in Zwelethemba, Worcester, in the early hours of Nov 21, killing four men and two women and wounding three, police said. The motive is under investigation.

Sources: EWN · IOL

Science & Space

Confirmed

BepiColombo enters orbit around Mercury

The European-Japanese spacecraft entered orbit around Mercury after an eight-year trip, making it only the second mission to orbit the planet after NASA's MESSENGER. Its two probes are due to separate in December, with the European orbiter reaching its final science orbit in March 2027.

Sources: Nature · Kyodo News
