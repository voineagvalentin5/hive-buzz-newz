France & Europe

Developing

France: CGT warns strikes will go on as Lyon transit walks out and the Yellow Vests plan a weekend of action

CGT leader Sophie Binet told RFI the government must make immediate, substantial announcements to end the protests, and her union has called another day of protest for Saturday, Nov 21. On Friday, Nov 20, a CGT strike shut three of Lyon's four metro lines, with line A running only in the afternoon. The Yellow Vests movement, now marking its eighth anniversary, is among the organisers of Europe-wide cost-of-living protests from Friday to Sunday.

Sources: RFI · Le Progrès · France 24

Developing

Europe-wide youth strike under way, with a general strike in Italy

Youth groups say young people in more than 25 countries are joining protests on Friday against the cost of living, poor learning conditions and military spending, under the slogan 'Fight for our future, not their wars'. In Italy, a 24-hour CGIL general strike hit schools, hospitals and local transport, and ANSA reported thousands marching in Rome, Milan, Naples, Turin and other cities. Air and rail are excluded from the CGIL strike, but a separate rail strike runs from 9 pm Friday to 8 pm Saturday, Italian time.

Sources: France 24 · ANSA · la Repubblica

Confirmed

Serbia: student-movement candidate Aleksandar Čupić elected parliament speaker

After days of delay, Serbia's new parliament elected Aleksandar Čupić, nominated by the Student List group, as speaker on Nov 19. He won 140 votes of the 239 MPs present, against 97 for Stojan Radenović, the candidate of President Vučić's group.

Sources: Government of Serbia (Tanjug) · Tanjug

Confirmed

Spain: race tightens before the Sánchez-Feijóo debate

A PF Global poll for Politico, taken Nov 15-17, puts the PP on 29.5% and the Socialists on 27.2%, a statistical dead heat, with Vox on 19.6%, leaving a PP-Vox bloc near half the vote ahead of the Nov 29 election. Sánchez and Feijóo held their head-to-head TV debate on Thursday night.

Sources: Politico Europe · El País

Confirmed

Albania: scuffles as police clear protesters from Tirana's main boulevard

On Nov 19, students and citizens again protested outside the prime minister's office in Tirana. Clashes broke out when police moved them off Dëshmorët e Kombit Boulevard to reopen traffic, and several women protesters were escorted away. Protesters say they will stay until Edi Rama resigns and reject any dialogue with him.

Sources: ATA · CNA

Developing

Belgium: De Wever restarts budget talks without Vooruit

As reported on Nov 19, Prime Minister Bart De Wever had held separate budget meetings on Tuesday and Wednesday with every coalition party except the Flemish socialists Vooruit, whose refusal stalled the last round. He is due to report back to King Philippe by Nov 23.

Sources: VRT NWS · Belgium Pulse

Confirmed

Ukraine hits the Crimean Bridge a third time; Putin promises a 'far more painful' response

Ukraine said it struck the bridge's rail section with Neptune missiles on Wednesday night, a day after bringing down a central road span. Trains were halted and later partly restarted, and Russia reported no injuries. Putin said he had been considering local ceasefires until the road span was destroyed, but that after the strikes Russia would not negotiate and would respond in a 'far more painful' way.

Sources: The Guardian · Reuters (via Euronext) · BBC

Berlin summit: Europe says it is ready for talks with Russia, but not a 'Yalta format'

At the Coalition of the Willing meeting in Berlin on Nov 19, Merz, Burnham, Macron and Zelensky said Europe has a united negotiating position and must be part of any deal. US envoy Jared Kushner said Moscow is now 'comfortable' with Article 5-style security guarantees for Ukraine, but Zelensky said Putin has always said no. Putin said Russia had invited Europeans to Moscow to set out their positions.

Sources: BBC · The Guardian (live)

Kyiv: daytime drone attack damages the Dutch and Austrian embassies and St Volodymyr's Cathedral

Russian drones hit Kyiv's historic centre on Nov 19 while the Shenzhen and Berlin summits were under way. One person was killed in Podilskyi (the Guardian says a 26-year-old man), and the Dutch foreign minister said a projectile struck behind the embassy, causing minor damage and no injuries.

US & Canada

Confirmed

Senate Republicans block a war powers resolution on Iran

The Senate voted 48-51 on Nov 19 against a measure directing Trump to end the Iran war or seek Congress's approval. It was the first such vote since Democrats won both chambers in the midterms. Republicans Collins, Murkowski and Paul voted for it, and Democrat Fetterman against. Democrats say they will keep pressing, including in talks to avoid a December shutdown.
