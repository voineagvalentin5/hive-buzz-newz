France & Europe

Developing

France: blockades tick up as Thursday's national strike nears

On Tuesday morning the education ministry counted 48 blocked high schools, up from 41 on Monday, and 51 of 184 higher-education institutions disrupted. Student unions claim far more. Marches took place in Paris, Lyon, Rennes and Marseille, and Paris police banned a student march route toward the employers' federation headquarters.

Sources: franceinfo · Euronews · BFMTV

Developing

France: unions and police still at odds over Thursday's Paris route

Unions want Thursday's cost-of-living march to run from Bastille to Concorde, but police had not approved the route as of Tuesday. CGT leader Sophie Binet criticised the delay, and authorities fear a repeat of the Oct 17 riots.

Sources: Le Parisien · France 24

Confirmed

France: attempted murder probe after petrol bomb hit gendarmes

Paris prosecutors opened an attempted murder investigation after explosive devices were thrown at about 15 gendarmes and police during Saturday's protest. No one had been arrested as of Sunday.

Sources: Dawn (agencies) · RTÉ

Confirmed

Europe: youth protests linked by shared money and housing worries

Reporting from France 24 and the Washington Post describes the youth movements in France, Germany, Spain, Italy, Belgium and Portugal as driven by housing costs, low pay, military spending and climate fears. In Germany, 54 people were arrested in Frankfurt after looting, and the interior minister promised severe penalties.

Sources: France 24 · The Washington Post · inews

Developing

Poland: Tusk and Nawrocki clash over the constitutional court

PM Donald Tusk says the dispute ends if President Karol Nawrocki recognises judges elected by parliament. Nawrocki calls the suspension of the court's president a forcible takeover. Police and protesters gathered outside the court.

Sources: Euronews · PAP · Polskie Radio

Confirmed

Serbia: arrests and resignations follow the students' election win

Since the Student List's Oct 25 victory, two senior police chiefs and a bank owner have been detained, and the bank's licence was revoked. Executives at public broadcasters RTS and RTV have resigned or been dismissed, and students are demanding more resignations in the police and courts.

Sources: AP · RFE/RL

Developing

Albania: Tirana student protests reach day 22

Students marched between faculties on Tuesday, urging a class boycott and demanding PM Edi Rama resign, though some faculties resumed teaching. Rama again offered talks, which protesters rejected. Police fined 16 people for blocking roads on Monday.

Sources: ATA · CNA.al

Confirmed

Ukraine: Russian bombs kill dozens at an aid centre in Zaporizhzhia

Russian aerial bombs hit a school used for aid handouts in Komyshuvakha, then a nearby shelter. Regional authorities put the toll at 29 on Tuesday, while Al Jazeera reported 31. Overnight, Russia hit energy sites, and a strike on a Kharkiv medical facility wounded 25.

Sources: AP · Al Jazeera · France 24

US envoy Steve Witkoff said Ukrainian and Russian delegations will meet in Abu Dhabi on Nov 10 and 11 to discuss security arrangements, humanitarian steps and ending the war.

Sources: Al Jazeera · France 24
