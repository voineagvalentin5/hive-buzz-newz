France & Europe

Confirmed

France: government raises the minimum wage 1.6% after Monday social conference

After a social conference with unions and employers on Monday, the labour minister announced the minimum wage will rise 1.6% on Dec 1, to 1,502 euros net a month. Prime Minister Lecornu said the rise alone is not enough and promised fast sector-by-sector wage talks, while unions had been asking for far more.

Sources: Le Parisien live · Le Monde live · Reuters via StreetInsider

Developing

France: teachers and students call a new strike and Paris march for Tuesday

Education unions and student groups have called a nationwide strike for Nov 10, with a Paris march proposed from Place de la Bastille at 2 pm Paris time (8 am ET). Police had not approved the route as of Monday morning, and Lecornu was due to meet student representatives Monday afternoon.

Sources: The Local France · Sortiraparis · franceinfo live

Confirmed

Spain: rail strike Tuesday, general strike Wednesday

Train drivers at Renfe strike for 24 hours on Tuesday, then CCOO and UGT hold a general strike on Wednesday under the slogan wages, housing and prices. The government set minimum services of 73% for high-speed and long-distance trains on Wednesday, and the election is on Nov 29.

Sources: Renfe · Euro Weekly News · Business Travel News Europe

Confirmed

Albania: daily Tirana protest reaches day 162 after Saturday's clashes

Protesters gathered again outside the prime minister's office on Monday, rejecting Edi Rama's offer of dialogue and also demanding that the opposition step aside. A European party group says students have challenged new protest restrictions at the Constitutional Court.

Sources: Aktualitet · Panorama · Bloomberg

Developing

Germany: Gen Z protests move to university strikes

Student strikes and demonstrations over living costs, education and health are being announced across Germany, following last weekend's violent rallies. This item rests on a Monday report and a recent Guardian overview, so details may change.

Sources: taz · The Guardian

Confirmed

Ukraine: strikes on both sides ahead of Abu Dhabi talks that open Tuesday

Russia hit Ukraine's power grid while Ukraine struck the KINEF oil refinery near St Petersburg, per ABC News. Rubio said talks will cover post-war security guarantees, and Trump said he expects the war to end soon, with Ukraine ending up with 'a little bit different shape'.

Sources: ABC News · The Guardian · AP

Rubio called some Iranian demands 'outrageous and ridiculous' after Tehran proposed cutting the gap before talks restart from seven days to two. Axios reports Trump asked Netanyahu to move forward on the Gaza peace plan in a post-election call. The roadmap covering disarmament and a stabilization force has been on hold for months, and Netanyahu remains skeptical that Hamas will demilitarize.

Sources: Axios · Arab News
