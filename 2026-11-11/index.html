Spain: first general strike in 14 years over the housing crisis

A 24-hour nationwide walkout backed by the UGT and CCOO unions began at midnight on Wednesday, with roadblocks around Barcelona and scuffles between pickets and police near Madrid's main wholesale market. Unions reported 90% early participation in the car and port sectors, while employer groups said overall turnout was low; the strike comes less than three weeks before the Nov 29 snap election.

Sources: Al Jazeera (AFP/AP) · France 24 (AFP) · NBC News (Reuters) · ABC (Spain)

Developing

France: education march stays calm in Paris as an official is hit with petrol in Chelles

Police counted 6,000 marchers in Paris on Tuesday and organisers said 10,000; the ministry said 49 high schools (1.3%) were blockaded and 1.6% of teachers struck. Outside a high school in Chelles, a regional councillor was hit by a bottle of petrol, prosecutors opened an attempted-murder inquiry, and Macron said violence has no place in a democracy.

Sources: franceinfo · RFI · France 24 (AFP)

Confirmed

Ukraine: Abu Dhabi talks end with a deal on a large prisoner exchange

After two days of US-mediated talks, Kushner and Russia's Medinsky both said the sides agreed on a large prisoner swap; Kushner said the numbers would be finalised on Thursday. He said there was progress on ceasefire monitoring and that he and Witkoff will meet Putin in Moscow on Thursday, while Medinsky was cool on any European role.

Sources: The National · Guardian live

Russia: drone attack sets Baltic oil port of Ust-Luga on fire

Leningrad region governor Aleksandr Drozdenko said a drone attack overnight caused a fire in the Ust-Luga port area and injured three workers, and that 150 drones were shot down over the region. Estonia, Latvia and Lithuania issued drone warnings and the blaze was visible from Estonia's coast.

Sources: Reuters via MarketScreener · ERR News
