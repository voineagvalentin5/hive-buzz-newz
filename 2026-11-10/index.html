France & Europe

Confirmed

France: Paris blames Iran's Revolutionary Guards for the supermarket fridge cyberattack

Foreign Minister Jean-Noel Barrot said an IRGC cyber unit called Shahid Kaveh remotely sabotaged refrigeration at more than 700 French supermarkets on Oct 11-12, spoiling perishable food. France summoned Iran's ambassador on Tuesday, will push for EU sanctions, and Paris prosecutors are seeking international arrest warrants for two of the unit's officials.

Sources: Al Jazeera · France Diplomatie

Developing

France: education strike day, with 49 high schools blockaded Tuesday morning

The education ministry counted 49 of about 3,700 high schools blockaded at 9:30 am Tuesday as teachers and students struck again. Police banned two student marches from Bastille, and the main Paris march leaves the Pantheon for Place d'Italie at 2 pm Paris time (8 am ET). Unions have called a public-sector strike for Nov 17.

Sources: Nouvel Obs live · franceinfo live · Sortiraparis

Confirmed

G7 in Paris: European ministers press Rubio on Ukraine and Iran

G7 foreign ministers met in Paris on Nov 9-10. European ministers pressed Rubio to make sure any Ukraine settlement is fair to Kyiv, and Germany's foreign minister said the talks would look for ways to avoid a new military escalation with Iran. Rubio said the Republican midterm losses will not change the administration's foreign policy.

Sources: AFP · AP

Confirmed

Ukraine: US, Russia and Ukraine open two-day talks in Abu Dhabi

The first three-way talks since February opened Tuesday, with Witkoff and Kushner leading the US side and Kremlin aide Medinsky leading Russia's; Kushner said they would focus on post-war security concerns. Control of the Donetsk region remains the core dispute, and the BBC says expectations of a breakthrough are low.

Sources: BBC · Kyiv Independent

Confirmed

Spain: train drivers' strike shuts several Madrid commuter lines, general strike Wednesday

Renfe said minimum services were not being met in Catalonia on Tuesday, and Cercanias Madrid said lines C2, C3, C4, C5 and C8 were not running. The 24-hour general strike over wages and housing follows on Wednesday, with more rail strikes on Nov 12 and Nov 17.

Sources: elDiario.es (EFE) · RTVE · Renfe

Confirmed

Albania: daily Tirana protest continues as police fine participants

Protesters gathered again outside the prime minister's office on Monday, as they have every day since May 31, demanding the Rama government resign. Police have started fining people for joining what they call an illegal gathering, and the investigative network BIRN reports police have issued multiple fines and taken peaceful protesters to police stations.

Sources: Panorama · Panorama (BIRN report) · Albanian Telegraphic Agency

Confirmed

Greece: migrants found dead on boats off Crete

Greece's coast guard recovered 12 bodies and rescued 18 people from one boat south of Crete, and one survivor later died in hospital, per Al Jazeera; the BBC puts that boat's toll at 13 or more. Al Jazeera counts at least 21 dead across boats trying to reach Crete.

Sources: Al Jazeera · BBC

Saudi authorities say an attempted attack on Nov 8 damaged an Aramco building in Riyadh's financial district with no injuries, blaming falling debris. The BBC links it to fresh Houthi strikes.
